Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

LittleWorks INC ("LittleWorks", "we", "us") operates littleworks.ca. This page explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and who to contact with questions. This policy is intended to align with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

Information We Collect

The only personal information collected through this site is what you voluntarily enter in the "Get Started" contact form: your name, email address, and message.

The contact form does not submit your information to our servers or any database. Instead, it opens your own email application with a pre-filled message addressed to hr@littleworks.ca. You choose whether to send that email. If you do, it is handled like any other email we receive.

Cookies and Tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party trackers of its own.

Hosting

littleworks.ca is hosted on Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify's infrastructure may log standard technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security and performance purposes. This is governed by Netlify's own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you email to us is used only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal information you've sent us by emailing hr@littleworks.ca.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hr@littleworks.ca.
